Yen Studio

What Yen Studio sends

Your songs and samples stay on your computer. There's no account and no analytics.

The app

  • Checking for a new version: one request to GitHub at each launch, for the latest release's number. It carries no identifiers. Nothing installs itself.
  • Models: Extract stems and Convert to notes download their models from GitHub the first time you say yes to it. Your audio is processed on your Mac and isn't uploaded.
  • The assistant: if you use it, what you ask and the parts of your song it reads go to Anthropic or OpenAI through your own Claude Code or Codex, under that service's terms.
  • Reports: Help › Report a Problem opens a GitHub issue in your browser, with the app's version and your system's filled in. You see it before you send it. Logs are never attached for you.
  • Local server: the app talks to its own engine on your computer and refuses requests from web pages.

This website

It's a plain page on GitHub Pages: no cookies, no analytics, and the fonts are served from here. GitHub keeps its own server logs, under its privacy statement.
