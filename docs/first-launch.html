Yen Studio

The first launch

macOS asks once, the first time you open Yen Studio. After that it opens like anything else.

  1. Open the download and drag Yen Studio to Applications.
  2. Open it. macOS says it can't verify the app: choose Done.
  3. In System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and choose Open Anyway.

Yen Studio is pre-alpha, so expect things to break. When something does, report it.
